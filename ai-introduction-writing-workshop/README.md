# AI-Assisted Research Introduction Workshop

This repository contains materials for a workshop on writing scientifically rigorous research introductions using generative AI as a **critical auditing tool**.

The goal is not to replace student writing.  
The goal is to teach students how to:

- strengthen research logic
- identify weak claims
- test evidence discipline
- clarify research gaps
- improve scientific reasoning
- revise intentionally

AI is used here as a skeptical reviewer — not as an authority, not as a ghostwriter.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this workshop, students will learn how to:

- structure a research introduction clearly
- separate claims from evidence
- identify overgeneralization and weak reasoning
- frame defensible research gaps
- translate critique into revision steps
- use AI responsibly in academic work

This workshop treats AI as a mirror for critical thinking, not a shortcut.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Student Instructions)

1. Open the audit prompt in the `prompt/` folder  
2. Copy the entire prompt exactly  
3. Paste it into your AI tool  
4. Replace the placeholder with your introduction  
5. Run the audit once  
6. Read the output carefully  
7. Complete the worksheet  
8. Revise your introduction

Important:

- Do not shorten the prompt
- Do not remove sections
- Do not ask the AI to summarize
- Do not ignore evidence warnings

The structure is intentional.

---

## 🧠 Pedagogical Philosophy

This workshop is designed to teach **scientific skepticism**.

Students practice:

- claim discipline
- evidence evaluation
- logical coherence
- conservative reasoning
- measurable research framing

AI is framed as a structured peer reviewer that forces clarity.

Students remain responsible for:

- accuracy
- citations
- originality
- ethical scholarship

---

## ⚖ Academic Integrity

This repository supports revision and critical evaluation.

It does not authorize:

- ghostwriting
- fabrication
- plagiarism
- citation substitution
- replacing original thought

Students must ensure all claims and references are independently verified.

AI feedback is advisory, not authoritative.

---

## 🧩 How Instructors Can Use This

Instructors may use this repository to:

- run a workshop
- assign a guided revision activity
- teach research writing structure
- introduce responsible AI usage
- model peer-review thinking

The materials are modular and adaptable.

---

## 🔁 Suggested Classroom Workflow

1. Instructor explains introduction structure
2. Students draft an introduction
3. Students run the AI audit
4. Students complete worksheet reflection
5. Students revise
6. Peer discussion of changes

This reinforces metacognition and revision literacy.
