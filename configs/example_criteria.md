# Synthetic demonstration criteria — version 1

Include empirical studies on artificial intelligence in education published in 2020–2025.
Exclude work outside education, non-empirical commentaries, and studies without relevant outcomes.
Title/abstract screening should err toward full-text assessment when information is insufficient.

Full-text exclusion reason labels:
- Wrong population/context
- Wrong study design
- No relevant outcomes

These criteria are an example, not a validated protocol for a real review.
